Topic: YouTube thumbnail generator on Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image)

Two things you should know before building:

1. A spec on this already existed (youtube-thumbnail-generator-nano-banana-pro.md, 7 June, never built). I replaced it rather than leaving a near-duplicate — it's still in git history if you want it back. It had a real error: it capped references at 6 total. The actual limit is 14 — 6 high-fidelity objects + 5 character-consistency + 3 style references. Those are separate budgets that behave differently, and that changes the design enough that reference handling is now its own component in both specs.

2. I split it in two, because your consistency requirement turned out to be bigger than one spec. Part 1 is the working generator (upload → 16:9 → download). Part 2 is where "keeps the same consistency" actually lives.

Research — the three tools I looked at (Pikzels, Canva, 1of10) converge on something the old spec missed. They've all moved past per-upload references to persistent consistency:

- Personas — save 3 photos of yourself once, every future thumbnail reuses them. No re-uploading.
- Styles — trained on 3 of your existing thumbnails, so new ones match your channel.
- Recreate — Pikzels' flagship: paste a YouTube link, it rebuilds that thumbnail's format with your face. This is the closest existing thing to "bring in an image and it recreates it," so Phase 3 of Part 2 is modelled on it.

Also picked up: scoring (Pikzels Score™ across virality/clarity/curiosity/emotion), title generation, and 1of10's mobile/dark-mode legibility check — that last one is cheap and genuinely useful, so it's in Part 1.

One design call worth flagging: Recreate describes then rebuilds rather than feeding the source image in as a style reference. It sends the reference to a text model for a composition breakdown (framing, colour, text placement), shows you that breakdown editable, then generates from it. Slower by one call, but it means you're recreating a format rather than copying an image — and it keeps the feature clear of reproducing someone else's face or logo.
